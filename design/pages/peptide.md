## Peptide `/peptides/<name>/`

- **FDA warning letters by substance** (owner task 2 Nov 2026; site.letter_substances): the section "FDA warning letters" says "<N> FDA warning letters name <Name>." and shows up to 10 newest in the letters page's table (date; company with the letter's first mention in `small` `ink-2` under it, in quotes; "FDA letter", `rel="nofollow"` to fda.gov), then "See all on the letters page" to `/fda-warning-letters/?sub=<slug>`; with none, "No FDA warning letter we track names <Name>.". The short answer adds "<N> FDA warning letters name it." (a link to the section) when N > 0. In quotes, the FDA's short bracket marks lose their brackets ("eye[,]" shows "eye,"). Without `letter_substances` the section links the letters page as before.
- **FDA listing** (when site.substance_status has `fda_listing`): the FDA's own wording from the same row as the status shown (the winning row's wording), in `small` `ink-2` under the status sentence (only when the substance has one current row; with two or more the wordings live in the facts card alone, owner answer Q3 b, 3 Nov 2026), here, in the "Is it legal today?" tables and in the search line ("FDA listing: \"…\""); without the column, nothing.

- **Several FDA listings** (owner task 2 Nov 2026): a substance with more than one current status row (today GHK-Cu, GHRP-2, GHRP-6, ibutamoren, ipamorelin, kisspeptin-10) shows every one in the facts card, newest first. The short answer keeps the top-ranked sentence and adds "The FDA lists it in <N> places; see the facts." (a link to the facts card) when N > 1. The "Is it legal today?" tables keep one row per substance.
- **Thin pages** (owner task 2 Nov 2026; a page with Search Console impressions is noindex all the same, owner answer Q4 b, 3 Nov 2026; ODOBRENO SEO - Karlo - 3. 11. 2026): a /peptides/<slug>/ page is indexed only when it carries at least three of six: a goal page that lists it, a shop table with at least one row, a clinic plan, an FDA warning letter that names it, a guide, an approved brand. Otherwise the page is still built and linked (from /peptides/, the legal tables, search and compare pairs), but carries `noindex` and leaves the sitemap and llms.txt, as /search/ does. The build prints the noindex list; `tools/check.py` reads the six off each page and fails a thin page that is indexed or a full one that is not.

Builds the Peptide page type above from data. A page exists for every substance with a status: every substance in site.substance_status (`legal_db`) that has a legal status, plus a substance whose own hand-written "Is it legal today?" row carries a status (retatrutide, Matrixyl, Argireline); a substance with neither gets no page (sermorelin has a page since its status is in the database). A missing fact drops its block; nothing is typed in to fill one. No dose anywhere on the site (`tools/check.py`) and no link to a shop. A clinic row carries the table button by the paid link rule (parts.md, Buttons): `btn-action` "Visit <Clinic>" with "Paid link" through `/out/<name>/` when the clinic has a paid link, else `btn-wait` "See plans" to our page on it; no other link to a clinic. No Product or Drug schema (the brand page is the one place for Drug, new-types.md).

- **The page in one screen** (owner task 3 Nov 2026, audit 11.3, B14, B15; ODOBRENO DIZAJN and ODOBRENO SEO - Karlo - 3. 11. 2026): the order is (1) the first screen, (2) the full table, (3) the blocks, (4) What to do next and Sources. - **The blocks** (`pep-body`, 8 + 4 from 900px): left, Evidence by goal and then the FDA warning letters, each a `panel-line` (`pep-block`: the heading at display-4, the lede in small, no rule); right, the Facts card and under it the Guides list. - **What to do next** (before Sources, two halves with Sources): the goal page ("Compare every option for <goal>"), every compare pair with the substance ("Compare side by side: A vs B"), and "Every seller of <Name>, with its checks" (/sellers/#substance=<slug>). ## Substance page parts for the new types (owner queue block 6, step 3b, 8 Nov 2026; waits for ODOBRENO DIZAJN)

- **Lab mark** (nav-parts.md): in the shops table's Checks cell, after "N of 5 checks", on a listing with a lab report we hold.
- **Price history** (nav-parts.md): in the blocks' left column after Evidence by goal, for the lowest price per mg at the shops shown, or for an approved medicine the cheapest plan that is not compounded; left out without enough points.
- **Clinics on a substance that is not an approved drug** (sermorelin, and any pharmacy-made substance with clinic plans): "Where to get it" lists the three cheapest clinic plans first, each with its "Compounded" chip and the table button (paid Visit when the clinic has a link, otherwise See plans), then the line "Also sold by N research-use shops; we do not link to them." to the shops table. The full table is the clinic table first, then the shops table under its own heading: shops and clinics are never mixed in one table (every-page.md). The status line stays the substance's own status ("Not approved"), never the clinic's words.
- **A plan for several medicines** (owner decision 0d, 8 Nov 2026): a plan that covers more than one medicine (a bundle, "Finasteride + Minoxidil") shows on each medicine's page, with one `small` `ink-2` line under the plan name: "One plan for <A> and <B>; the price is for the whole plan." It counts once per page and once per goal page, and it is never the "lowest price" of either medicine alone.
- **Brand pages** (new-types.md, Brand): when a brand page is indexed, the brand's name in the table and in "Approved brand" links to it.
