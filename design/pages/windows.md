# Windows

The seller (shop) window and the clinic window. Index: [`design/pages.md`](../pages.md).

## Seller window (shop window)

- **Seller window, on demand** (owner UX audit, 3 Nov 2026; ODOBRENO SEO and ODOBRENO DIZAJN - Karlo - 3. 11. 2026; owner answers Q1 a, Q2 a, Q3 a): Home carries no window in the page. "Prices at N shops" loads the window from `/windows/<key>.json` on the first click and opens it; without JavaScript the button opens the substance page's shop table (`/peptides/<slug>/#sold`, else /sellers/). Goal pages keep their windows inline. A cosmetic option's window lists only cosmetic forms (cream, serum, lotion, gel, topical; "topical" from 3 Nov 2026, owner answer), titled "Cosmetic shops", each row with its form word before the size ("Serum · 30 ml", "Topical · size unknown"); a research-use window lists none of them, and a cosmetic window never a vial (`tools/check.py`). A cosmetic option with no cosmetic listing gets no window and the grey line "Sold as a cosmetic; no prices checked." where the button would be. An option whose window lists research-use shops but whose `how` names none says "Research-use shops; we do not link to them." (plus "Online clinics also prescribe it." when a plan carries the substance). An FDA-approved row that needs no prescription (its `how` says so: minoxidil) labels its button "Pharmacy" (the audit asked for "Pharmacy, no prescription", which does not fit the 160px button; the how line already says "No prescription").
- **Seller window** (`<dialog class="sellers-dlg">`, one per substance, components in `bundle.css`; owner, 31 Oct 2026, from `mockups/sellers-10.html` and "we compare, we never sell"): a window over the page, `white`, `radius-panel`, at most 1040px wide, the page behind it dimmed and not scrolling; focus stays inside; it closes with "Close" (`btn-line btn-sm`, the only button in it), Esc or a click outside, and focus goes back to the button that opened it. Full screen on phones. A cosmetic window (creams and serums) has no Per mg column and no line about price per mg (owner task 3 Nov 2026). Inside, top to bottom: for a row with one `substance`, the substance's display name as the heading; for a row with a `substances` list (a row that stands for several research-use peptides: "Injectable peptides sold for hair" = BPC-157, GHK-Cu, TB-500; "CJC-1295 / ipamorelin"; "PT-141 from online shops" = bremelanotide), the option's name as the heading and one table per substance under the substance's name as a sub-heading (`.dlg-sub`), N counting distinct sellers across them; a pharmacy-made row ("Compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide") has no substances and no button. Otherwise (its "Is it legal today?" row name when that row is for it alone, e.g. "BPC-157", else the database `canonical_name`, else the shortest option name that carries it; never the slug); "Research-use sellers. We do not link to them. Prices checked <newest `last_seen`>."; a DataTable, one row per seller, sorted by the seller's score (high first), then the number of products it lists (`product_count`, more first), then name, a seller without a score last (owner, 2 Nov 2026; each seller's listing is still its cheapest by price per mg, and price per mg stays in its column): seller name as a link to our page on the seller, `/sellers/<slug>/`, when it has one, else plain text, and never a link to the seller's own site (owner, 31 Oct 2026; owner task 2 Nov 2026); product name with its size in mg; price; price per mg (tabular figures; "blend" for a blend, "unknown" when the amount is not stated); and the background check: first the **seller score** (owner, 2 Nov 2026; components in bundle.css, Seller score), "N of 5 checks" with the number in tabular figures and the five checks as small marks (11px) on one line from 641px, in this order: Lab reports, No FDA letter, Found in a state registry, Legal pages, Fresh prices (met `chip-ok`; not met `chip-neutral` with a 1px dashed `ink-muted` border, so a missed check never rests on colour alone; owner answer Q1 a, 2 Nov 2026; the state also spoken for screen readers; "Lab reports" is about the seller, owner answer Q2 a; from `score` and the `score_*` yes/no fields of site.sellers; a seller without a score shows no line), then the six-point background check as six small chips: COA for this listing (`chip-ok` "This product: COA yes (n)" or `chip-warn` "This product: no COA"; owner answer Q2 a, 2 Nov 2026, so it never reads against the seller's "Lab reports"), FDA warning letters (`chip-ok` "none" or `chip-warn` with the count), registered company (`chip-ok` with name and state, or `chip-neutral` "Company: not verified" when no registry match exists), seller status (`chip-ok` "active"), first seen (month and year) and price last checked (date), both `chip-neutral`. A missing value says "not found" or "unknown", never a blank. Under the table: "Price per mg is price divided by the stated amount; blends have no per-mg price." and "Checks are facts we hold about the seller, not a safety or quality grade. Checked <date>." (`score_checked_on`, the newest in the window; the line is not drawn when no seller has a score). Never the words "safe", "trusted" or "best" about a seller in the window (`tools/check.py` fails the build on them). and the window's source list: each seller's product page as text, not a link, with the date the price was checked. No Visit, no paid link, no `<a>` to a seller anywhere in the window (`tools/check.py` fails the build on one). Under 640px the table becomes stacked cards, one per seller, the score line under the seller name and the marks wrapping. Data rule: `tools/pull_data.py` keeps, per substance shown on the site, each seller's cheapest listing and then the ten cheapest of those, plus the `sellers` rows they point to; numbers only from there; em and en dashes in sellers' product names show as hyphens.

## Clinic window (landing v3 package, owner decisions 4 Nov 2026; ODOBRENO SEO and ODOBRENO DIZAJN - Karlo - 4. 11. 2026)

**Clinic window.** The button on an option with clinic plans reads "Buy from $X/mo · N clinics" ("· N sellers" and the caption "Pharmacy, no prescription" for a medicine sold without a prescription). N is the clinics in the window. $X is the row's own plan: on an FDA-approved row its cheapest plan that is not compounded, so a compounded price never stands on an approved row; on the compounded row, the cheapest compounded plan; on the Cheapest card, that card's plan. The same button stands on Home, the goal pages and the decision cards. It opens `/windows/clinics-<goal>-<option>.json`; without JavaScript it opens the goal page's clinic table, or the plan's clinic page on a goal without one.

The window lists every clinic with a plan for the option: tile 28 and the clinic's name (our page), the drawing and the plan with a brand's substance in brackets, the kind chip, form, per month with the FDA letter chip and "Closed by FDA" when the data has them, care fee, "N of 4", `btn-sm btn-wait` "See plans" to our clinic page, or `btn-sm btn-action` "Visit <Name>" with "Paid link" once the data carries the clinic's paid link. Brand plans first, then compounded, each cheapest first. A brand approved for another use on this goal stays out (Ozempic on weight loss; it stays in the goal page's table). Under the table: "Prices are the clinics' published monthly prices on <date>. A clinic with an FDA warning letter is still listed; the letter is public record." A research-use or cosmetic option keeps its shop window; a window never mixes clinics and shops. Phones: rows become cards.

## Order of sellers and clinics (owner, 11 Nov 2026, queue block 33; ODOBRENO DIZAJN - Karlo - 11. 11. 2026 - PR #182)

Option rows (Home, goal pages and subpages): group 1, rows whose button reaches at least one clinic with a paid link; group 2, rows not FDA approved (compounded, cosmetic, research-use) without one; group 3, every other row. Inside a group: the best score among its paid clinics, then the lowest monthly price, then the order in data; `order_options` sorts before any page is drawn and data/site.json keeps its order. Home no longer says "FDA-approved options first" (owner answer Q2 a).

The clinic window and every list of sellers or clinics (goal and substance clinic tables, /sellers/ clinics, Home's seller list, "Where to get it", "Similar sellers", group pages, the offer page's "Other clinics"): sellers with a paid link first, by their five-check score high to low; then sellers without one, by score high to low; the list's own order (brand before compounded, cheapest first) breaks a tie. A sort the reader picks still wins. Research-use shop windows keep their rule: by score, high to low, never a link. Unchanged: "Cheapest elsewhere" (one pick, named cheapest), /compare/ columns (the H1's order) and the guides' price tables, which stay cheapest first (owner answer Q1 a). `tools/check.py` (check-paid-first) fails a seller without a paid link above one with a paid link in a default list.

## Paid links open in a new tab (owner, 12 Nov 2026, queue block 35, PR #186)
A paid link (`btn-action` through `/out/`) opens in a new tab: `target="_blank"`, `rel="sponsored noopener"`, `aria-describedby="new-tab"`, with one hidden "Opens in a new tab" per page. Nothing visible changes: no icon, no extra word on the button. Our own links stay in the same tab.

## State filter (owner task 5 Nov 2026)

**"Your state"** is one control in the filter row above every clinic table: goal pages, peptide pages, the /sellers/ clinics tab and the clinic windows; on phones the same control at full width above the cards. A select of the 50 states and DC, two-letter code with the full name, first option "All states". A chosen state hides each clinic whose `site.seller_states` excludes it and says what we know: "1 of 34 clinics does not ship to California", "2 of 34 clinics do not ship to ...", and "No clinic here is known to skip <State>" when none does ("seller" in a window of medicines without a prescription), counted in clinics, not plans (owner answer Q1 b, 5 Nov 2026; ODOBRENO DIZAJN and ODOBRENO SEO - Karlo - 5. 11. 2026). A clinic with no exclusion data counts as serving every state. The choice stays in localStorage (try/catch), applies on load and in a window opened later, never in the URL, and leaves only through the `filter_change` event (`filter` state, `value` the code or `all`). Without JavaScript the control is hidden and every row shows. Script: `STATE_SCRIPT` in the cached `nav.js`.
