# Shared parts

The parts more than one page uses: buttons, option rows, tiles, drawings, the body figure, chips and the logo. Index: [`design/pages.md`](../pages.md).

## Buttons: one rule each

| Button | Only for | Label pattern | Max per |
|---|---|---|---|
| `btn-action` (green) | A paid outbound link to a partner, through `/out/<name>/` with `rel="sponsored"` | "Visit <Clinic> · $X/mo", "Visit <Clinic>" | one per table row or option row; always with "Paid link" under it, in a table or window inside it as its second line. Before a clinic pays us there is no green button at all: `btn-wait` to our own page takes its place, and no page says "No paid link yet" (Home v2, 3 Nov 2026; `tools/check.py`) |
| `btn-ink` | The site's own main action | "Compare hair options", "Search" | one per section; one in the header |
| `btn-line` | A second choice next to an ink or action button, or a quiet action alone | "Read our Hims review", "How we rate", "Prices at 10 shops" (opens the seller window) | one per group |
| `btn-sm` | Any of the above inside a table row | as above | one action + one line per row |
| `btn-mark` | The email alert band (not built yet) | "Get price alerts" | one per page |
| `btn-wait` (white, `line-strong` border) | Our own page on a clinic that has no paid link yet (Home v2, 3 Nov 2026); it becomes `btn-action` "Visit <Name>" once the clinic pays us | "See plans", "See plans at Strut Health · $25/mo" | one per table row or option row; no caption in a table or window, "Our clinic page" under it in a list outside a table |

**The paid link rule** (owner decision, 4 Nov 2026; ODOBRENO PRAVILA - Karlo - 4. 11. 2026): an FDA warning letter does not by itself block a paid link; the letter stays visible next to every price and on the seller page. A paid link stays blocked only for research-use shops (always) and for a seller whose letter is about adulterated drug products while the FDA has published no close-out letter. Why: a warning letter is a notice the company often corrects (the FDA then issues a close-out letter); a publisher's liability in the US comes from its own claims, and this site makes none. Research-use shops stay unlinked: the product itself is an unapproved drug. Compounded plans follow the same rule (owner answer Q1 a, 4 Nov 2026; this replaces the letter part of decision C8; the "Best compounded plan" card keeps its button to our clinic page). `site.paid_links` applies the rule; a page draws `btn-action` whenever the data carries a link, on the kinds of seller that may have one; `tools/check.py` fails a `btn-action` on a research-use shop's row or page, or on a seller with an Adulterated letter and no `closed_on`.

**The table button** (owner task 4 Nov 2026; ODOBRENO DIZAJN - Karlo - 4. 11. 2026): in every table and window, `btn-action`, `btn-wait` and `btn-line` are one part in another colour: 14px, 8px side padding, left edges aligned in the column, `cta-width` (232px) from 900px and the column's full width below. It is `touch` (44px) tall at every width; on phones it takes the card's full width. Nothing goes under a table button. The green one carries "Paid link" inside it as a second line under "Visit <Name>", centred, white at 85%, 12px (13px on phones, the 13px floor in `tools/check_layout.js`). From 641px a row has 12px above and below the button, so a row with "See plans" and a row with "Visit" look the same (68px) and its cells are centred on the button; the hairline between rows stays (owner task 4 Nov 2026).

**One pair of actions per page.** The site's own action is `btn-ink`; the paid click is `btn-action` with "Paid link" under it (in a table or window, inside it). A text link never repeats the button above it. No link to a partner anywhere except through a `btn-action`.

Everything else is a text link in `action` green. A partner without an affiliate program gets `btn-line` "Read review", never green. A "research use only" seller gets no button at all: one row with a `chip-neutral` "Not approved", no price, no link.

## Option rows (shared by Home and the goal pages)

**Option rows** (Home step 2 and the goal pages share them; Home v2 step 7, 3 Nov 2026; the landing v3 package replaces their layout, the button and "· 3 clinics": see Pictures and the clinic window). Collapsed, a row shows the name with its chips, then one line: the option's `plain` sentence (data/site.json, goals[].peptides[].plain; Tier 1) and its price from data in bold: "From $25/mo at online clinics · 3 clinics" (the row's clinic plan: on a goal with clinic plans as before, on any other goal the substance's cheapest plan that is not compounded; with the plan's footnote and the clinic's FDA letter chip), "From $1.14/mg at 10 shops" (the lowest price per mg in its window, inside the $0.01 to $100 band), "From $48 at 4 shops" (a cosmetic window), else the hand price with its footnote. One button per row: a row with a clinic plan, `btn-wait btn-sm` "See plans at <cheapest clinic> · $X/mo" to our clinic page with "Opens our clinic page" under it ("Pharmacy, no prescription" for a medicine that needs none), or `btn-action` "Visit <clinic> · $X/mo" through /out/ once the data carries the clinic's `paid_url`; a research-use row, `btn-line btn-sm` "Prices at N shops" with "Our checks, no shop links"; a cosmetic row, `btn-wait btn-sm` "Compare N serums" ("products" unless every listing in its window is a serum; owner task 3 Nov 2026) opening its cosmetic window, or the line "Sold as a cosmetic; no prices checked." without listings; otherwise the row's `no_link` line, or nothing. A goal none of whose rows has a clinic or cosmetic button (Muscle, Healing, Energy) ends its list with "What is approved instead: <guide>" from `approved_instead_guide`; no clinic row is invented. On Home the rows never say "we do not link" (the label legend says it once). Opened, the row keeps its does, status and how lines. Home holds only the first goal's rows; the others load from `/windows/goal-<slug>.json` when chosen (without JavaScript only the first goal shows, as before). On phones the button sits under the row with its small line beside it (phone sketch).

## Pictures and the clinic window (landing v3 package, owner decisions 4 Nov 2026; ODOBRENO SEO and ODOBRENO DIZAJN - Karlo - 4. 11. 2026)

**Option rows, one structure.** The product drawing; the open control (32 by 32 with a neutral tile, chevron 16px, turning from right to down, the whole row header opens it) and the name; the badge line (status chips and the FDA letter chip together); one line with the price and its footnote. The button sits in a column `cta-width` (232px) wide from 900px, label centred, the same `btn-wait` on every row; a caption under it only when it carries a fact ("Pharmacy, no prescription", "Our checks, no shop links"), centred. The button by kind of row (owner, 4 Nov 2026): FDA approved with clinic plans, "Buy from $X/mo · N clinics"; sold without a prescription, "Buy from $X/mo · N sellers"; cosmetic, "Compare N products"; research-use, "Prices at N shops" with "Our checks, no shop links", on every goal, opening the shop window with our checks. A row whose data has no listing says so in the button's column, by its kind, never an empty space: sold without a prescription, "Any pharmacy, no prescription"; on prescription with no clinic plan, "On prescription; no clinic prices yet"; cosmetic, "Sold in creams and serums; no prices checked"; research-use, "No listings yet". `tools/check.py` fails anything else. A medicine sold without a prescription shows only plans that are one too: no compounded plan and no pill (owner answer Q5 a). A compounded plan of that medicine gets its own compounded row under it, with its own clinic window (`plan_substances`; hair: "Compounded minoxidil spray"). With no plan that fits, the goal page's "No prescription" card shows the medicine's checked price and "Any pharmacy, no prescription". Rows are at least 96px tall from 900px. At 900px and under the button is full width under the text. The goal's intro always shows in full, the width of the panel; it is never cut.

**Seller tile.** One part, `parts.tile`. Square, radius 22% of the size. **A real logo appears only for an approved affiliate partner whose kit we hold; every other seller shows a monogram (owner answer Q1 b, 4 Nov 2026).** So three cases: real logo, clinic monogram, shop monogram. Sizes: Home seller table 40, goal page clinic table 28, clinic window 28, /sellers/ 40, seller page H1 72, "Cheapest elsewhere" 24, "Similar sellers" 28, /compare/ column heading 40, phone seller cards 36. Option rows carry no tiles. A real logo only with `logo_path`, `logo_source` and `logo_checked_at` in the data, from a brand kit or a press kit made for this use, never from a website, a favicon or an image search: white tile, 1px `line`, 12% padding, `object-fit: contain`. Until then a monogram at 35% of the size, weight 800: the first two letters of the short name, or the first letters of its first two words; trailing words like Health or Clinic do not count. Online clinics take one of `tile-1` to `tile-10` by a hash of the slug. A research-use shop is always grey (`paper-2`, `ink-2`), never a logo and never a brand colour. Hidden from screen readers, because the name always stands beside it. **A real logo beside a clinic's H1** (owner answer Q3 a, 4 Nov 2026; ODOBRENO DIZAJN - Karlo - 4. 11. 2026): where the seller page has the H1 tile, a clinic with a real logo shows `.h1-logo` instead: the file itself, 28px tall, its own width up to 140px, above the H1 on phones, no frame, no link, empty alt because the name stands beside it. A monogram stays at 72. The owner adds the file to `assets/logos/<slug>.svg|png`, and `data/seller_logos.json` holds its source and date; the file without the entry fails `tools/check.py`, the entry without the file draws the monogram.

**Product drawings (owner answers Q1 c, Q2 c, Q3 c, Q4 a, 4 Nov 2026).** Clinics get the seller tile above; every product gets a drawing, our own, never a seller's or a maker's picture and no package art. Eight, in `assets/art.svg`, on a 56 grid, one 2-unit stroke and two tones: the outline in the kind's colour, one light fill; no gradients, shadows or faces.
- One mapping (`parts.plan_drawing`, `option_picture`): compounded `vial-compounded`, brown (`art-compounded` tile); research-use `vial-research`, grey (`art-research`); cosmetic `serum-dropper`; pill, tablet, capsule or oral `pill-bottle`; pen, KwikPen, autoinjector or shot `pen-brand`; foam, liquid, solution, spray or topical `foam-bottle`; cream, gel or lotion `cream-tube`; serum `serum-dropper`; vial or injection `vial-brand` (all `art-tile`).
- An approved option takes `drawing` from its data when set (Egrifta is a vial kit, with its source), else the form its own lines name, else the form of the row's plan.
- `tools/check.py` fails an option row or plan row with no drawing, except a plan whose data names no form at all, which keeps the space.
- Sizes: Home option rows 56, goal option rows and decision cards 52, tables and the window 40, phones 48; the drawing fills 72% of its tile. Radius `radius-art`, `radius-art-s` at 40.

**Goal drawings.** Seven, in `assets/art.svg`, each in its goal's colour: tokens `goal-hair`, `goal-skin`, `goal-weight-loss`, `goal-muscle`, `goal-recovery`, `goal-libido`, `goal-longevity`, a dark line (`value`) on a light panel (`tint`), at least 4.5:1. The goal photos below take their place; a goal without a photo entry falls back to its drawing, at 48 on the Home card (34 on phones) and 120 on the goal panel. No picture of either kind in the By goal menu or the "Compare other goals" buttons (owner answers Q5 a, Q6 b).

**Goal photos (owner pick, 5 Nov 2026).** One real photograph per goal, so a beginner sees what the goal is about.
- **Where:** the Home "Compare by goal" cards (panel 84 tall, 60 on phones) and the goal page panel (300 by 220 beside the H1 from 900px, not drawn below it). Nowhere else: no heroes, option rows, seller pages or guides. Product drawings stay drawings.
- **Frame:** close crop, 3:2 source, calm light, one subject a beginner recognises at the phone card size.
- **Never:** a person's face, a product or its packaging, a brand or readable logo, a needle in skin, a before-and-after, anything suggesting a result. Never an AI image of a person.
- **Treatment, the same for all:** 3:2 crop at 900 by 600, saturation 72%, contrast stretched between the 1st and 99th percentile, a 12% wash of `ink`. Files `assets/photos/goal-<slug>.jpg` and a 450 by 300 `-450` version for the Home cards. The panel's own radius; `object-fit: cover`. No gradient, no text on the photo.
- **Sources:** only free sources whose licence allows commercial use without attribution (Unsplash License, Pexels License, CC0). `data/goal_photos.json` holds photographer, URL, licence and check date per goal; a file without its entry fails `tools/check.py`. An AI image only where no photo exists, marked as such in the data.
- **Alt:** empty, decorative, because the goal's name stands beside it.

## Logo

**The mark (owner decision, 2 Nov 2026):** two circles joined by two short bars, a peptide bond; the right circle is the chosen one. It keeps its own colours and is never redrawn, recoloured, rotated, shadowed or animated.

- **Construction** on a 56 by 56 grid: left circle centre (13, 28), radius 10; right circle centre (43, 28), radius 10; two bars from x 22 to x 34 at y 25 and y 31, 3 units thick, square ends. The mark itself spans x 3 to 53 and y 18 to 38 (50 by 20), and the files are framed on it (`viewBox="3 18 50 20"`), so a mark set at 20px tall is 20px of circle, not 20px of empty frame.
- **On light** (`assets/logo.svg`): left circle and bars `ink` (#0f1d2b), right circle `clinic` (#0b5cad).
- **On dark** (`assets/logo-dark.svg`, the ink footer and dark panels): left circle and bars `white`, right circle `mark` (#ffe066).
- **Clear space:** half the mark's height on every side; nothing else inside it.
- **Minimum size:** 16px wide.
- **Wordmark:** "Peptide Chooser" in Public Sans 800 (`font-brand`), sentence case, `ink` on white and `white` on ink, 10px from the mark (half the mark's height), on the same line, never stacked.
- **Sizes:** header mark 20px tall beside the name at 20px (18px tall beside 17px under 640px); footer mark 20px tall. Share images (`tools/og.py`): mark 32px tall beside the name at 32px, 16px apart.
- **Favicons** (`tools/og.py`, from `assets/logo.svg`): 16 and 32px on a clear background, the mark filling the 56 grid; the apple touch icon (180px) and the manifest icon (512px) on `white` with the clear space on every side. `build.py` links them and writes `/site.webmanifest`.

## Medical cues (only these)

- **The body figure** (replaces the drawn chart; owner task w4, ODOBRENO DIZAJN - Karlo - 4. 11. 2026). A realistic standing adult in a front view that reads as neither male nor female (owner, 4 Nov 2026), matte light clay with no skin detail, closed eyes and no anatomical detail, soft studio light, a faint floor shadow, on white. It is a pre-rendered image of our own (`assets/body/`, drawn by `tools/render_body/` from the CC0 MakeHuman model), never a stock photo, never organs. The box is twice as tall as wide, keeps the size the chart had at every width, and clips what it holds; a zoomed picture fades out softly at the box's edges so the body is not cut hard. Each goal has a pin: a white ring with a blue dot, filled blue when chosen, one size at every zoom. From 900px a pin is a 24px control; under 900px seven do not fit, so pins are markers and the figure and the goal rows are the targets. Choosing a goal (pin, click on the figure, or goal row) zooms to that goal's part of the body and shades it in a soft `clinic` blue glow, with a small white label beside the pin naming the goal. `btn-line` `btn-sm` "Full body", Esc, or a click on the figure goes back; the chosen goal stays. Skin and Energy and aging stay on the whole body: skin shows a blue rim around the figure and the face, energy and aging a light wash with a soft glow on the chest. Pins and goal rows are one control: choosing either updates both and step 2. The goal's area and zoom live in `tools/render_body/` (`regions`, `VIEWS`).
- **The flow** (Home hero, 31 Oct 2026). No card: the two numbered steps sit straight on the `white` first screen, left to right: Choose a goal, Compare the options. Each step opens with a 32px `clinic` circle with its white number (800) and an H2 (18px, 700) over a 1px `line` rule. There is no separate "Get it" step: where to get an option is on its own row (owner, 31 Oct 2026), so a seller comparison opens from that row, in a window over the page that the reader closes ("Close", Esc, or a click outside it). The window: white, 12px corners, at most 960px wide, `shadow-card`, a sticky head with the H2 and `btn-line` "Close", the page dimmed behind it with `ink` at 55%; full screen on phones.
