## Page anatomy

- **Notice bar** (`paper-2`, 36px, `small`): one sentence on what we are and how we earn, with a link.
- **Header** (72px, white, 1px `line` under it): wordmark, five nav items (44px tall), one `btn-ink`. Nav never wraps; on phones it scrolls.
- **Home hero**: the H1 on one line, then the flow on white (figure and goal list, then the options with where to get them); then, on the grey band, the lede, the How we rate strip and the trust links (pages.md, Home).
- **Category hero**: H1 and short answer left, price pad right (7 + 5).
- **Sections** on landing pages: 72px apart, each opened by a section heading (H2 in `display-2`, one-sentence lede, 1px `line` under it, "All X" link on the right).
- **Tables**: a white card with 12px corners and a 1px `line` frame, a `paper-2` header row in 13px `ink-2`, `line` between rows; prices in `number-l`.
- **Lists of short items** (Top picks, glossary): white cards with 1px `line` borders in a grid. **How we rate on Home** is one strip: the heading, then the five seller checks in a row, divided by 1px `line` rules, no cards.
- **Article pages**: 8-column body with `body` text at 64ch, H2 in `display-3` with a 1px `line` above, 4-column sticky sidebar with the decision card first.
- **Footer** (`ink`): wordmark and the one-line disclaimer.

## Schema.org (owner task 2 Nov 2026)

JSON-LD in build.py on every page: Organization (name, url, logo) and WebSite on Home; BreadcrumbList on every inner page; Article on guides; ItemList (one ListItem per row, name and url) on the letters page and on /sellers/ when it exists. `tools/check.py` parses every block and fails a page that lacks the type it needs.

## Rules that apply to every page

- **"legit", one exception** (owner decision 2 Nov 2026; for the rulebook with ODOBRENO PRAVILA): "legit" stays banned as a claim; the only allowed place is a seller page's H2 "Is <Name> legit? What we can check", in question form, and the fixed closing sentence under it. `tools/check.py` fails the word anywhere else and tests both cases on every run.

- **Shops and clinics are not mixed** (owner task 2 Nov 2026; peptide-data decision 26): a shop lists products (`product_count > 0`), an online clinic only plans (`product_count` 0). Every list of shops takes shops only: the "Prices at N shops" windows and their counts, Home's "Sellers we track" ("shops"), the shop table and counts on /sellers/, the seller list on /compare/ (clinics are there as plans), the guide counts ("shops we check"). In search a clinic is its own type, chip "Clinic", leading to its page. `tools/check.py` fails a build with a clinic inside a price window.

- **Author line** (owner task 2 Nov 2026): every inner page type (goal, seller, peptide, compare, guide, and the letters page) carries one line under the H1, the same pattern and class everywhere: "Peptide Chooser editorial team. Checked <data date>." (`p.small.meta`, the team name linked to /about/). The date is the newest data date the page shows: a goal page the newer of the hand check and the goal pages' stamp date; a seller its newest of prices, checks and first seen; a peptide `pep_checked`; a compare pair its newest plan or price date; a guide its build date; the letters page the last pull. This replaces the longer author lines described in the sections above ("Last updated", "Tracked since", "Prices checked", the standing line) and the Checked stamp beside the author on the compare pairs and the letters page; the compare pair's line on where its numbers come from stays as its own `small` line. On the goal and peptide pages "Every claim links to its source." follows as its own `small` line (owner answer Q2 b, 3 Nov 2026). A seller's "Tracked since <first_seen>" is a row at the foot of the Background check card's list, its name in `ink-2` in the chip column, no chip (it is not a check; owner answer Q1 b, 3 Nov 2026). `tools/check.py` requires the author line exactly once on each of these pages (ODOBRENO DIZAJN and ODOBRENO SEO - Karlo - 3. 11. 2026).
- **No orphan pages** (owner task 2 Nov 2026): every page in the sitemap is linked from at least one other page of the build (the sitemap and llms.txt do not count). `tools/check.py` fails a page that is not; the fix is the natural link (a compare pair from both its pages, a guide from /learn/, a seller from /sellers/).
- **No placeholders in public.** Nothing in square brackets ships. A fact that cannot be checked is left out or reads "To confirm".
- **Share image.** Every page gets an og:image drawn at build time from its title: mark, title in `display-2`, "Peptide Chooser" in `small`, on `paper`. 1200 by 630. Never hand-made. A page born from new data in the deploy gets its image there (`tools/og.py --missing`, then a second build); images drawn on a computer are committed as before (owner answer Q1 a, ODOBRENO PRAVILA - Karlo - 3. 11. 2026).
- **Numbers from data, never typed.** Prices, counts and FDA status come from the site's data file; the page only renders them, with their check date.
- **Tier 1 guard** (owner answer to #94 Q2, 5 Nov 2026): in the deploy, after the last build, `tools/tier1_guard.py` compares every page's title, meta description and H1 with the live page; data alone can change them. Words stop, numbers and data-born pages pass (owner answer, 5 Nov 2026), and every difference is listed old and new in the run log. Four things pass by rule: the same words with other digits, dates, prices or counts (a number inside a name, as in GLP-1, is a word; "No FDA warning letter" becoming "1 FDA warning letter" is words); a new page under /sellers/, /compare/ or /peptides/ whose title and H1 are ones a live page of that section already has, whose every description sentence one of them already uses, and whose name stands in the data and the URL; a page that left the build when its URL is now a redirect to a built page; a seller page's meta description made only of the build's fixed description sentences for that seller, differing only in the medicine list, plan count, price, care fee, letter count or checks met (owner, 5 Nov 2026; ODOBRENO PRAVILA - Karlo - 5. 11. 2026). Anything else stops the deploy, also a new goal page or guide, and a page gone without a redirect. It then passes only when `path hash` lines, as the guard prints them, stand under the owner's `ODOBRENO SEO - Karlo - <date>` in the commit message or in `data/tier1-approved.txt`. The hash is of the new text, so an approval covers that text and nothing later. A live page it cannot read also stops the deploy.
- **Scroll reveal never hides an anchor** (owner UX audit part 2, 3 Nov 2026; ODOBRENO SEO and ODOBRENO DIZAJN - Karlo - 3. 11. 2026): with a hash in the URL, or on a hash change, every revealed part shows at once; on load, what is already in or above the window shows without waiting.
- **"We do not link" once** (owner decision C1, 3 Nov 2026; audit C12; waits for ODOBRENO SEO): the phrase family ("we do not link", "we never link" and their variants) is said only where the rules require it: Home's label legend (once), a seller page's closing sentence under "Is <Name> legit?", and /how-we-rate/ and /disclosure/, where the policy is explained. Everywhere else a page says what it does instead ("Research-use sellers, with our checks.", "Each name opens its page.", "each name opens our page on that shop"). `tools/check.py` counts the family on every page and price window, prints the count per page and fails on any other occurrence.

## Measurement

Clicks in GA4, so our numbers can be compared with the networks' reports (owner decision, 5 Nov 2026). Nothing a visitor sees changes.

- **Where:** at the end of `/assets/nav.js`, the one cached script every page already loads, so no extra request and no new file. No library. `tools/check.py` (events) enforces the rules below.
- **Consent:** one gate, the page view's (`window.pcGA`, set only after "Accept"). Without consent an event is dropped, never queued. A failure in the script never breaks a click.
- **How:** listeners on `document`, so windows loaded on demand are covered. Parameters come from `data-ev-*` attributes the build writes (slugs and numbers). Never search text, an e-mail or a query string; a text filter sends the value "text", a search only its length. The address and referrer reach Google without `?q=`.
- **Every event:** `page_type` (on `<body data-pt>`: home, goal, seller, peptide, compare, guide, letters, sellers, search, other; /peptides/ and /learn/ are other), `page_path`, and the page's own `seller`, `substance` or `goal` where it has one.
- **Events:** `paid_link_click` (`seller`, `out_path`), `see_plans_click` (`seller`, `price_month`, `kind`, and `substance` on a plan row; a row that shows no one plan sends the clinic's cheapest), `prices_window_open` (`substance`, the first of an option's; "compounded" on a compounded clinic window; `window_kind`: research, cosmetic or clinic; `rows`), `decision_card_click` (`card`: best-approved, cheapest, no-prescription), `goal_pick` (Home chooser, By goal menu, body figure pin: `goal`), `filter_change` (`filter`, `value`), `tab_change` (`tab`), `legal_check` (`substance` or "none"), `search` (`query_length`, `result_type`: the chosen result's type, or "page" when the form is sent), `compare_pick` (`kind`: plan or seller; a pick on /compare/ or the seller page's Compare button), `outbound_fda` (every fda.gov link; `letter`, the letter's slug, or "none" on another FDA page), `newsletter_submit`.
