A shop is checked on five. A score is never compared across the two kinds." Everywhere a score shows, a clinic reads "N of 4" and a shop "N of 5": the seller page (top block, Background check card, legit list), /sellers/ (the check bar has four marks on the clinics tab; "Minimum checks" offers up to "4 of 4" there), Home "Where to get it", compare pairs of two clinics ("more of our four checks met"), search results, the About counts.
