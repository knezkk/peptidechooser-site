## Sources `/sources/` (owner task 3 Nov 2026)

Where every number on the site comes from. Title "Sources: where every number comes from | Peptide Chooser" (the 60-character title rule), H1 "Sources", one `lede` line, then one group per kind of source, each an h2 over a list of rows between hairlines (bundle.css, Sources): **FDA** (fda.gov pages, openFDA and DailyMed labels; the database's status lists once each, with how many substances it reads from them; the warning letters with their count and date span), **MedlinePlus**, **PubMed studies** (an h3 per guide citing them; a study no guide cites, under its page), **Price pages** (outside price pages the goal pages cite, never a seller in the database), **State registries** (the registry files the seller checks found companies in, with the sellers and the check date), **Our own database** (what the crawler reads: shop product pages, clinic plan pages, the seller checks, each with its count and newest date; the date of the copy the build used). A row: the source's name as its link (an outside link with `rel="nofollow"`; a clinic or shop page and a non-web address stay text), what we take from it ("Cited on <up to three pages> and N more pages", or the database line) and "Checked <date>" where the data has a date. Built from the source lists of the pages in the build and from the data, nothing typed; a group with no rows is left out. How often the crawler runs is not in the data, so the page does not say. Breadcrumbs (Home / Sources), in the sitemap and llms.txt. `tools/check.py` fails a group with no source.

## Paid link redirect page `/out/<slug>/` (owner task 4 Nov 2026; ODOBRENO DIZAJN - Karlo - 4. 11. 2026)

Not a designed page: no header, styles, fonts or analytics, and nobody should see it for more than a moment. One page per row of `site.paid_redirects` (`paid.py`; the data in `data/paid.json`, until the view exists `tools/fixtures/paid.json`). `noindex,nofollow`, `Disallow: /out/`, not in the sitemap. `location.replace()` and a `meta refresh` to the tracking address, and one line "Continue to <Name>", a plain link with `rel="sponsored nofollow"`. Click source: the path of the page on our site that sent the click (from `document.referrer`, same site only, no query, cut at 50 characters), as Katalys `aff_sub`, Awin `clickref`, CJ `sid`; FirstPromoter takes none. A tracking address appears nowhere else: not on a page, not in `site.json`.
