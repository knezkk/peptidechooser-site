### Compare any two `/compare/` (owner task 2 Nov 2026; ODOBRENO DIZAJN and ODOBRENO SEO - Karlo - 2. 11. 2026)

Any two online clinic plans, or any two sellers, side by side. Built from the Letters page's top and grey band, the Inputs and the new **Compare two** part. Not built without `plans` or `seller_profiles` in data/site.json (never drawn empty).

- **Title:** "Compare any two plans or sellers | Peptide Chooser". **H1:** "Compare any two plans or sellers". **Description:** "Put two online clinic plans or two peptide sellers side by side: monthly price, care fee, five background checks and FDA warning letters, from our data." Breadcrumbs: Home / Compare any two.
- **Top** (white): H1; `lede`: "Pick two online clinic plans or two peptide sellers and see them side by side: price and care fee for a plan; the five checks, products, price per mg and FDA letters for a seller. Seller names open our page on the seller; we do not link to the shops."; the Checked stamp (newest plan `last_seen` or `score_checked_on`) and, in `small`, "N clinic plans and M sellers."
- **The form** (`paper-2` band, one white card): two selects labelled "First" and "Second", each listing "Choose a plan or seller", then the group "Clinic plans" (every priced plan in `plans` as "<clinic>: <plan>", by clinic) and the group "Sellers" (every seller in `seller_profiles`, by name); `btn-ink` "Compare". The form is a GET to /compare/?a=...&b=... (a plan is `p.<seller_slug>.<plan_slug>`, a seller `s.<seller_slug>`), so a comparison can be shared; with JavaScript the page reads a and b on load, draws the result and keeps the URL in step on "Compare".
- **The result** (Compare two, under the card): two columns with the same rows. Plans: Per month, Care fee ("$99/mo", "None, all-in", "Not known"), What it is (the clinic table's kind chip, "Brand, diabetes" for Ozempic), Substance (its database name, "Names no medication" for a program), FDA letter (`chip-warn` link to the letters page with its date, else "None"), Prices checked. Sellers: Checks ("N of 5 checks" and the five marks of the seller window), Products, Substances, Per mg (from to, built only from listings between $0.01 and $100/mg: the seller's own lowest and highest and those of its top substances, so a misread unit never shows; owner, 2 Nov 2026, widening the seller pages' Q6 a rule; "Unknown" when none is left), FDA letters ("None", or the count with the newest date), Company (only a registry or FDA letter name, with where it was found; else "No registered company found"), Tracked since. Each column is headed by its name: a seller's name links to /sellers/<slug>/, a plan's clinic links there only when the clinic has a seller page; the line under it is the seller's kind or the plan's name. A plan and a seller together: "Choose two clinic plans or two sellers: a plan and a seller do not share the same facts." Nothing on the page links to a seller's or clinic's site (`tools/check.py`).
- Under it in `small`: "Checks are facts we hold about the seller, not a safety or quality grade." and "How we rate". With JavaScript off: "The side by side view needs JavaScript. Without it, open each seller from the sellers we track." (a link to /sellers/).
- In the sitemap, llms.txt and the share images like every page.
- **Pickers that search, and substances** (owner task w2, UX audit 7.4, B17, B18, 3 Nov 2026; waits for ODOBRENO DIZAJN and ODOBRENO SEO): with JavaScript each select becomes a text field ("Type a plan, shop or substance") with a list under it (the header search's list part: white, `line-strong` border, `radius-panel`, `shadow-card`; arrows, Enter, Escape), its rows grouped under a 12px `ink-2` label: **Online clinic plans**, **Research-use shops**, **Substances** (every substance with a /peptides/ page); names that start with what was typed come first. Without JavaScript the selects stay, with the same three groups ("Choose a plan, shop or substance"). Two substances with a pair page open that page; two without one draw two columns: FDA status (chip and sentence), Clinic plans from (the lowest plan per form, "Pen from $149/mo"), Lowest per mg ("$X/mg, lowest of N shops", "None shown"), FDA letters ("N letters name it"), Goal pages. A plan with a shop or a substance: "Choose two of the same kind: two clinic plans, two shops or two substances." The `lede` adds "two substances" ("Pick two online clinic plans, two peptide shops or two substances ... FDA status, prices and letters for a substance."); title and H1 unchanged. **Popular comparisons** lists only substance pairs (the tool cannot pair two clinics).

## Compare pairs `/compare/<a>-vs-<b>/` (owner task 2 Nov 2026; ODOBRENO DIZAJN and ODOBRENO SEO - Karlo - 2. 11. 2026)

- The "FDA letters" row shows each substance's count of letters that name it ("144 letters", linking the letters page filtered to it, "name it" under it), or "None we track names it" (owner task 2 Nov 2026).

Builds the Compare page type above as static, indexable pages from the data /compare/ uses. One page per pair: the two URL names in alphabetical order joined by "-vs-", never b-vs-a (`tools/check.py` fails a sitemap with a pair twice). No shop pairs. No link to a shop or a clinic.

- **Substance pairs:** every two substances with a /peptides/ page that sit on one goal page and have the same kind of status: both FDA approved, both not approved (Pharmacy-made counts as not approved), or both cosmetic (owner answer Q1 b, 2 Nov 2026). A pair on two goals is one page. Rows: FDA status (chip, status sentence, source link and date), Goal pages, Lowest price at shops ($/mg and the shop count, shops only; an approved medicine reads "Not shown: we list clinics, not shops, for an approved medicine"), Clinic plans from (the cheapest plan that is not compounded, approved medicines only), Forms sold, What the goal page says (the option's own line on the shared goal page), FDA letters (the letters page; letters are listed by company, not by substance), Guides.
- **Clinic pairs:** every two clinic pages that both have a compounded plan of the same substance (semaglutide, tirzepatide); a pair sharing both is one page with a row per substance. Rows: Compounded <substance> (the clinic's cheapest compounded plan, $/mo and the plan name), Care fee, FDA letter, Checks (the five marks), Company, Prices checked. At most 40 pages; over 40, only pairs where both clinics meet 2 or more of 5 checks. A pair already live (in the live sitemap the deploy reads) always stays, whatever the count; new pairs fill the rest up to 40, in order (owner answer Q2 a, ODOBRENO PRAVILA and ODOBRENO SEO - Karlo - 3. 11. 2026; rule 8).
- **Page:** breadcrumbs Home / Compare any two / <A> vs <B>; H1 "<A> vs <B>"; the short answer as the `lede`, two or three sentences from data (the statuses, or which is cheaper per mg or per month with the care fee; for clinics, the FDA letters); the Checked stamp with the author line and one line on where the numbers come from; in the `paper-2` band the /compare/ two-column table (static, the same part, stacked under 640px), the line "Our pages on each: <A> and <B>." and `btn-line` "Compare any two" to /compare/, prefilled `?a=&b=` with the two plans, only on a clinic pair or an approved pair; a not-approved or cosmetic pair has no button, since /compare/ holds plans and sellers only (owner answer Q2 b, 2 Nov 2026).
- **Title:** "<A> vs <B>: FDA status and prices | Peptide Chooser" (substances) or "<A> vs <B>: prices, care fees and checks | Peptide Chooser" (clinics), shortened to 60 characters: without the suffix, then "prices and checks", then "<A> vs <B>". **Description** 110 to 160 characters from the same facts. BreadcrumbList; canonical; share image; sitemap and llms.txt.
- **Links in:** /compare/ gains "Popular comparisons" under the form (the Related guides list part, up to 12 pairs, the most shops or plans first); each /peptides/ page and each clinic page gains one `small` line "**Compared with:** <pair>, <pair>." (`compared`; on a peptide page above Guides, on a clinic page at the end).
- **Decide first** (owner task w2, UX audit 7.4, B17, B18, 3 Nov 2026; waits for ODOBRENO DIZAJN and ODOBRENO SEO): under the author line, the verdict card (white, 1px `line`, `radius-panel`; the `h-sources` heading "Which to pick" and one or two sentences in 600), from data only, never an effect or side-effect claim. Each fact favours one side: the cheaper plan of a kind and form both sell (compounded injection with compounded injection, brand pen with brand pen; the like-with-like rule), the lower price per mg at the shops (research-use pairs), more clinics or shops selling it, fewer FDA letters naming it (research-use pairs only), a different FDA status. When each side has a fact: "Pick <A> if <fact>. Pick <B> if <fact>."; when one side has two: "Pick <A> if <fact>, or if <fact>."; one fact alone: "Same FDA status; the difference is <fact>." (clinic pairs: "The one difference we hold is <fact>."); no fact: no card. The facts read "price matters most: <kind> <form>s from $X/mo against $Y", "price per mg matters most: from $X/mg against $Y/mg", "you want more clinics (shops) to choose from: N against M", "you want fewer FDA letters naming it: N against M", "you want an FDA-approved medicine", and for clinics "you want a clinic with no FDA warning letter", "you want more of our five checks met: N against M". The card carries its fact count (`data-facts`). **Rows:** an approved pair has no "Lowest price at shops" row; the "FDA letters" row becomes one `small` line under the table, "FDA warning letters we track: N letters name <A>, M name <B>. These letters are about sellers and advertising, not about the medicine's approval." **What to do next** at the end of the band (the guides' link list): substance pairs the two /peptides/ pages ("<Name>: FDA status and prices"), the goal page ("<Goal>: every option compared") and "Sellers of <A> we track" (`/sellers/#substance=<a>`); clinic pairs the two clinic pages ("<Clinic>: plans, prices and checks"), the substance pages and /weight-loss/. `tools/check.py`: a verdict states two facts or none, never one; no "Not shown" row; the tool's pickers offer both sides of every built pair.
- **Checks** (`tools/check.py`): both sides link to their /peptides/ or /sellers/ page; a substance pair carries both status sources; no dose; no seller links; no pair twice in the sitemap.
- **Like with like** (owner UX audit part 2, 3 Nov 2026; ODOBRENO SEO and ODOBRENO DIZAJN - Karlo - 3. 11. 2026): a clinic pair's price row shows the plan and its form ("Compounded Semaglutide · Injection") when both clinics sell the substance in the same single form; otherwise each side lists one line per form ("Injection from $70/mo", `.cmp-form`). The verdict compares one form both sell and names it ("For compounded semaglutide as an injection, …"); with no common form it calls neither cheaper and gives each side's price and form, or "form not stated".
