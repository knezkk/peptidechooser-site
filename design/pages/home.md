## Home `/`

Home is the hub for every goal: built from the 26 Oct 2026 mockup, rebuilt on 3 Nov 2026 from the owner's "Home v2" sketch (design canvas "Peptide Chooser: gotov Master 1", boards "Početna v2" desktop and phone). Its title and layout are set once and never change when a category is added; a new category only adds a card, a panel and rows. Every section under the hero ends with its one action (`.sec-act`); `tools/check.py` allows at most one `btn-ink` and one `btn-line` per section outside its tables (the letters section's pair). The page stays under 60 KB (the price windows load on demand, w1's UX audit). "We do not link to them" and its variants are said once on Home, in the label legend (`tools/check.py`).

- **The two steps** (inside the hero; every goal is on the figure and in the chips from day one, a goal is never "coming soon"; with JavaScript off the first goal is shown):
  1. **Choose a goal**: the body figure and one goal row per goal (all seven, `label` and "N options"), the chosen row `clinic-tint`; pins and rows are one control. Click, Enter or Space choose; arrow keys move to the next or previous row and choose it.
  2. **Compare the options**: at the right of the step heading, "Is it legal today?", linking to `#legal`. Then the chosen goal's `for` sentence, then the option list: one row per option in the goal's `peptides`: the name and its `does` line (footnotes for any number); the status chip (database status when there is one, else the hand-written one; a cosmetic product keeps its `chip-info` "Cosmetic, no approval needed", because the database status is for the substance as a drug, owner 31 Oct 2026) with `status_plain` under it; the price "From $X/mo" with its source and check date, only where the option's price has a source, at the end of the `how` line (owner, 1 Nov 2026); after it, on the row of the plan's substance, the goal's online-clinic plan line (**Online-clinic plan line** (owner, 31 Oct 2026): a goal's substances are the `substance` of its options (hair: finasteride, minoxidil). From `plans` (site.plan_prices, pulled on every build) the cheapest `price_monthly` among them, leaving out plans whose `product_kind` is `compounded` (a pharmacy-made copy is not the approved product the line sits under; those belong in the seller comparison, marked Pharmacy-made), shows as "Online clinics from $X/mo (Seller)", the seller name without its legal name in brackets, with a footnote to the plan's `pricing_url` and its `last_seen` date in the numbered source list. A goal with no plan row shows nothing. The Checked stamp, where one is shown, uses the newest `last_seen` of the shown plans when it is newer than `prices_checked_on`.) in data/site.json (the goal's `price_source` for the option marked `price`, or the option's own `price_source` with `price_from_usd`); then the `how` line. Then `btn-ink` "Compare <goal> options" for a goal with a page; for an unpublished goal the existing sentence "Full comparison for this goal is not published yet. The legal status above is current as of <date>."
  Every FDA-approved option row (status FDA approved, not marked `top_pick: false`; owner, 1 Nov 2026: each approved medicine shows where to get it, e.g. finasteride and minoxidil both) ends with `btn-sm btn-action` "Online clinic" and "Paid link" under it, or the disabled span with "No paid link yet" until the goal has an affiliate url. A goal with no FDA-approved option has no Visit button; its research-use rows already say we do not link to them. **Prices at N shops** (owner, 31 Oct 2026; every research-use row, 1 Nov 2026): every option row whose `substance`, or any substance in its `substances` list, has rows in `products` (data/site.json, from site.products) and that is not FDA approved (hand status or database status; owner, 31 Oct 2026: an approved medicine's row never points to research-use sellers) gets `btn-line btn-sm` "Prices at N shops" in its action column, N the number of sellers listed for that substance ("1 shop" when one). **The top-pick rule** (retired with the Home compare table, 3 Nov 2026; kept here as the record). The top pick is the first option in the goal's `options` (or, for a goal without a comparison page, its chooser options) whose status is FDA approved and that is not marked `top_pick: false` (an option approved for another use, like tesamorelin for muscle); if none, the option marked `best`; if neither, the row says "No FDA-approved option" and has no "Online clinic"; under it the goal's most asked-about peptide: the first option with a not-approved status (database chip when there is one, else the hand-written one) that research-use shops sell, as name, with its status chip in the FDA status column; no button, no price. Under the name, as a note: "Sold by research-use shops; we do not link to them." (owner answer Q5 b, 1 Nov 2026). Every approved chip on Home reads "FDA approved"; prescription is stated in the option's "how" line. No price is typed. The button is disabled with "No paid link yet" under it until the goal has `affiliate` (`name`, `url`) in data/site.json; then it is `<a class="btn btn-action" href="/out/<name>/" rel="sponsored">` with "Paid link" under it. **Compare any two** and the **email alert band** are added when the compare pages and the email service exist, never drawn empty.
