## Sellers `/sellers/`

The index of the seller pages (owner task, phase 4d, 2 Nov 2026; online clinics first, owner task w2, UX audit 2.6, B7 and 11.5, 3 Nov 2026, waits for ODOBRENO DIZAJN and ODOBRENO SEO). Built from the Letters page's parts: header, breadcrumbs (Home / Sellers), the white top with the Checked stamp, the grey band, DataTable, the Legal filter, the Home seller block's segmented control (`seg`). Not built without `seller_profiles` in data/site.json (never drawn empty).

- **Title:** "Peptide sellers and their checks | Peptide Chooser". **H1:** "Peptide sellers we track" (both unchanged, rule 8). **Description:** as approved (ODOBRENO SEO - Karlo - 2. 11. 2026).
- **Top** (white): H1; one `lede`: "<N> online clinics that prescribe and ship, then <M> research-use shops. Every name opens our page on that seller; checks are facts we hold, not a grade." (the counts are the rows of the two tables); the Checked stamp (newest `score_checked_on`).
- **Two tabs** (`paper-2` band): the segmented control (`seg`, `seg-b` with `aria-pressed`) **Online clinics (N)**, the default, and **Research-use shops (M)**. One table shows at a time. The tab is in the hash, `type=clinics` or `type=shops` (Home's "See all" link sends `type=clinic` or `type=shop`; both are read), read on load and on hash change. Without JavaScript the control is hidden and both tables show, clinics first, each under its own h2 ("Online clinics", "Research-use shops"; with JavaScript the tab names the table and the h2 stays for screen readers and the page outline).
- **Filters** (one block above the tables, built from the Letters filter's parts; owner answers Q1 a, Q2 a, Q3 a, 2 and 3 Nov 2026): "Filter by name" with `btn-line btn-sm` "Clear"; **Substance** ("Any substance", then every substance the shops list or the clinics' plans carry, most shops first, "<Name> (N shops, M clinics)", the name without its bracket), **Minimum checks** (Any, 1 or more ... 5 of 5) and **Sort** (the shops: Most checks, Most products, Lowest price per mg, Name, Tracked since, oldest first; the clinics: Most checks, Lowest monthly price; the select shown is the open tab's); then "Checks met" and the checks as chips in plain words, each with the number of sellers on the open tab that meet it: **Lab report this year**, **No FDA letter**, **Company found**, **Legal pages found**, **Prices this month** (the lab report chip is hidden on the clinics tab: a clinic never has one). The `aria-live` line: "N of M clinics match." or "N of M shops match." while a filter is set. "Clear" empties the name, substance, minimum and chips (not the sort or the tab). With no filter the shops show the first 25 in the chosen order and `btn-line` "Show all N"; the clinics show all. Under 640px each label sits above its select at full width.
- **Online clinics table** (`id="clinics"`, cards under 640px, labels from `data-l`): **Clinic** (a link to its page), **What it prescribes** (the plan medicines by name, the first three and "and N more"; "Not named" when its price page names none), **From** (the lowest monthly price, "$X/mo"), **Checks** ("4 of 5" and the five checks as a bar of five miniature marks: the Background check card's chips at 10px, met filled with `chip-ok`'s text colour, not met `chip-neutral` dashed, each spoken), **FDA letters** ("None", or the `chip-warn` "N letters, <newest date>" as on Home), and the clinic's one action: `btn-sm btn-wait` "See plans" to our page on it, `btn-action` "Visit <Name>" with "Paid link" only when data carries a paid link (Home's rule, `clinic_button`). Under it in `small`: "Clinics prescribe and publish no lab reports, so a clinic meets at most 4 of the 5 checks." A clinic that excludes states has the "Your state" control in the filter block instead of a "Not available in" line (windows.md, State filter).
- **Research-use shops table** (`id="shops"`): **Shop**, **Sells** (its substances by name, most listings first, the first three and "and N more"), **From** (the lowest price per mg between $0.01 and $100/mg, the Compare page's rule, "unknown" when none is left), **Checks**, **FDA letters**, and `btn-sm btn-line` "Our review" to our page on it. Under the tables: `btn-line` "Compare any two" to /compare/, and in `small` "Checks are facts we hold about the seller, not a safety or quality grade." with the link "How we rate".
- **Hash rule:** the state lives in the URL hash, only the values that differ from the default: `type` (shops), `q`, `substance`, `min`, `check` (comma list of lab, letter, company, legal, fresh), `sort`, `csort`, e.g. `/sellers/#type=shops&substance=bpc-157&min=3`. Read on load and on hash change (an unknown value falls back to the default; a hash without "=", `#clinics` or `#shops`, opens that tab), written with `replaceState` on every change. /sellers/ stays one URL; the canonical does not change.
- **Checks** (`tools/check.py`): every seller page in the build has its row in exactly one of the two tables and none is hidden in the HTML; the clinics table comes first in the HTML; the lede's counts and the tab labels equal the rows; no old jargon chip label ("Lab report in 12 months", "Fresh prices"); the filter block and the tabs carry `hidden`; the substance options and the scripts name only what the rows carry; a peptide page's /sellers/#substance= link names an offered substance. `tools/check_layout.js`: both tabs at 320 to 2560px.
- **Filter fold** (owner task 3 Nov 2026, the 390px walkthrough; bundle.css, Filter fold): under 640px the filter block folds behind one `btn-line btn-sm` "Filter" (aria-expanded); the match count stays visible under it. From 641px the button is not drawn and the block shows as before.
