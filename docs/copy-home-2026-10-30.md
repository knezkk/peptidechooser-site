## 1. Jedna rečenica na vrhu početne (ispod H1, prije chooser‑a)

> **What is a peptide?** A short chain of amino acids. Some are approved medicines (semaglutide, bremelanotide), some are cosmetics in creams, and many are sold online with no approval and no testing. This site tells you which is which, what it costs, and what the proven alternative is.

## 2. Paneli po cilju: naslov + „for whom” rečenica + opcije (što radi · kako do toga · status)

### Hair
- **Naslov panela:** Hair growth and hair loss
- **Lista ciljeva:** Hair loss
- **Rečenica:** For thinning hair, a receding hairline or bald spots: what slows the loss, what regrows hair, and what is only marketing.
- **Finasteride 1 mg** · A daily pill. Slows hair loss and regrows some hair in most men; not for women who may become pregnant. · Prescription, from an online clinic or your doctor. · FDA approved for hair loss. From $10/mo (generic, pharmacy coupon).
- **Minoxidil** · A liquid or foam you put on the scalp twice a day. Regrows hair for men and women; works while you keep using it. · No prescription, any pharmacy. · FDA approved for hair loss. From $10/mo.
- **GHK‑Cu copper peptide serum** · A cosmetic serum for the scalp. Small lab and follicle studies; no large trials in people. · Sold in shops, no prescription. · Cosmetic: legal to sell, nobody checked that it works.
- **Injectable peptides sold "for hair"** · Vials from research‑use shops, meant to be injected. No human evidence for hair. · We do not link to them. · Not approved for any use.

### Skin
- **Naslov:** Skin: wrinkles and firmness
- **Lista:** Skin
- **Rečenica:** For fine lines, loss of firmness and dull skin: what a prescription cream does, and what a peptide serum can and cannot do.

## 3. Rječnik ispod chooser‑a (jedna kartica, naslov „What the labels mean”)

- **FDA approved** : the FDA checked that it works and is safe for this use, and every batch is inspected. Prescription unless marked otherwise.
- **Cosmetic** : legal to sell without approval; nobody has to prove it works. Fine on your skin, no claims beyond looks.
- **Not FDA approved, pharmacy‑made** : a licensed pharmacy mixes it for you on prescription. Legal in limited cases; no batch testing by the FDA.
- **Not approved** : no approved product exists. Shops sell it "for research". Buying it is not a crime for you, but nobody has checked what is in the vial. We never link to these shops.
- **Is it safe?** : only approved products have safety data from large trials. For everything else the honest answer is: unknown.
